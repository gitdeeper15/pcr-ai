## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-04

### Initial Program Manuscript

First public release of **The Production and Control of Representations in Artificial Intelligence — Research II: A Conceptual Architecture, Authority Structure, Representation Lifecycle, and Research Protocol**.

This is a research protocol, not an empirical study. No findings are reported.

### Added — Research Manuscript

- `PUBLICATION/Production_Control_Representations_In_Artificial_Intelligence.pdf` — full manuscript (22 pages).
- Four conceptual figures:
  - Figure 1: Five functional units (Producer → Model → Platform → Interface → Recipient).
  - Figure 2: Six lifecycle stages — canonical path.
  - Figure 3: Five authority types.
  - Figure 4: Five structurally distinct lifecycle paths.

### Added — Central Question

- Who produces the representations a recipient encounters through AI systems, who controls their formation, availability, and appearance, and who consumes them?

### Added — Foundational Documents

- **Conceptual Architecture** (v0.2, adopted) — five functional units, five foundational distinctions, five configuration patterns.
- **Authority Structure** (v0.1, pending) — five authority types, five pairwise distinctions, five distribution patterns.
- **Representation Lifecycle** (v0.1, pending) — six stages, canonical path, five alternative paths, structural breaks.
- **Research Questions** (v0.1, pending) — 20 questions across four families (A–D).

### Added — Methodological Commitments

1. Function ≠ Owner — functions are analytical, owners are empirical.
2. Authority ≠ Function — authority is the right to do; function is what is done.
3. Canonical path is a reference point, not a norm.
4. No methodology is fixed in advance (RQ-D2).
5. Research I and Research II remain methodologically independent (RQ-D1).

### Added — Open Research Questions

- OQ1: Whether the five functional units are analytically distinct in actual systems (RQ-A1).
- OQ2: Which lifecycle paths appear in practice, and with what frequency (RQ-C1).
