# Figures — Research II

This directory contains the four conceptual figures accompanying the manuscript. Each figure corresponds to a structural element of the architecture.

---

## Figure 1 — The Five Functional Units

**File:** `fig1_five_functions.png`
**Source:** Section 3.2 of the manuscript.
**Description:** A chain showing the five functional units — Producer → Model/Generative System → Platform/Controller → Distribution Interface → Recipient/Consumer.
**Note:** The five functions are analytical, not presumptive organizations or technical components. A single entity may perform more than one function; a function may be jointly performed by several entities.

---

## Figure 2 — The Canonical Lifecycle Path

**File:** `fig2_lifecycle_canonical.png`
**Source:** Section 5.3 of the manuscript.
**Description:** The six stages of the representation lifecycle in sequence — Origination → Transformation → Selection → Distribution → Reception → Re-origination.
**Note:** The canonical path is analytically clean but empirically rare; it serves as a reference point rather than a normative description.

---

## Figure 3 — The Five Authorities

**File:** `fig3_five_authorities.png`
**Source:** Section 4.2 of the manuscript.
**Description:** Five authority types — Production, Transformation, Selection, Distribution, Interpretive — each with its central question.
**Note:** The five authorities are analytically distinct; a single entity may exercise more than one, and an authority may be jointly exercised or distributed across entities.

---

## Figure 4 — Five Structurally Distinct Lifecycle Paths

**File:** `fig4_lifecycle_paths.png`
**Source:** Section 5.4 of the manuscript.
**Description:** Canonical, Merged, Skipped Transformation, Distributed Origination, and Iterative Re-origination paths.
**Note:** The canonical path serves only as an analytical baseline; the five alternative paths are offered as structurally distinct reference points expected to be more empirically common.
